Loupe Jewels | Exquisite Handcrafted Gold & Certified Lab-Grown Diamond Jewellery

Welcome to Loupe Jewels (Loupe Jewellery LLP), your premier Indian destination for certified lab-grown diamonds, hallmarked 14K and 18K solid gold jewellery, and bespoke heirloom craftsmanship based in Surat, Gujarat.

Core Jewellery Collections

  • Diamond Rings: Solitaire engagement rings, eternity bands, and everyday sparkle set in yellow gold, rose gold, and white gold.
  • Diamond Earrings: IGI certified diamond studs, drop earrings, huggies, and festive chandbalis.
  • Fine Necklaces: Solitaire diamond pendants, bridal chokers, and delicate gold chains.
  • Tennis Bracelets: Classic diamond tennis bracelets and custom-fitted bangles.

Ethical Diamond Craftsmanship & Certification

Every diamond in our collection is sustainably cultivated using cutting-edge CVD and HPHT technology, offering identical physical, chemical, and optical brilliance to mined diamonds with zero ecological conflict. Each jewel is certified to international standards (IGI & GIA) and crafted with 100% BIS hallmarked gold.
